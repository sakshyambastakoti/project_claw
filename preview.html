COMMAND CENTER • HARDWARE FLEET ARCHITECTURE

Project CLAW is a high-torque wearable animatronic mechanism with 6 claw/arm assemblies actuated by dual 12V 100RPM Johnson geared DC motors. Motor 1 contracts and deploys the claws via a red cable network, while Motor 2 retracts them via an independent blue cable network.
